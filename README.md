## 💫 About Me:

Hi, I'm **Ajinkya**! I'm an Information Technology student who stepped into data analytics out of curiosity — and fell in love with it. I don't just report numbers, I focus on understanding the **why** behind them.

- 🔭 **Currently working on:** Building and refining projects like my Retail Sales Analysis using SQL, Python, and Power BI
- 👯 **Looking to collaborate on:** Open-source data analytics tools, complex SQL optimization, and collaborative data engineering pipelines
- 🤝 **Looking for help with:** Advanced data storytelling and real-world internship opportunities to grow alongside experienced professionals
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** The latest advancements in AI-driven data insights, and expanding my skills as a certified SQL AI Developer Associate
- 💬 **Ask me about:** SQL, Python, Power BI, Excel, or how to translate raw data into actionable insights
- ⚡ **Fun fact:** I won a competition for developing a simplified gaming app for children in a community engagement field project!
